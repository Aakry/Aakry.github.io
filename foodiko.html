FOODIKO

this page has foods cooked and/or tasted by me.

cooked by me

Menemen

Type: breakfast, eggs, quick

Concept: the "Sunday morning" dish. Pan-cooked tomato and pepper, eggs scrambled soft, served straight from the pan with bread.

Tasting notes: sweet tomato, a little heat, soft eggs. Best when the eggs are still loose.

Churn ideas: add feta, try chili flakes, make a spicy version, or serve in a small cast iron pan for a photo.

Photo ideas: overhead shot, steam rising, a spoon in frame, bread torn beside it.

Rating: 4/5 (keep)

Mercimek çorbası (lentil soup)

Type: soup, cheap, weeknight

Concept: the comfort food that fixes a bad day. Red lentils, onion, carrot, and a squeeze of lemon at the end.

Tasting notes: earthy, slightly sweet from the carrot, bright with lemon. Mint butter on top works well.

Churn ideas: blend it smooth, add cumin and paprika, or serve with crispy bread croutons.

Photo ideas: close shot of the steam, lemon wedge on the side, a dark spoon for contrast.

Rating: 4/5 (keep)

Homemade pizza

Type: dinner, project, friends

Concept: the "one more try" food. A long dough rest, a very hot oven, and whatever toppings are in the fridge.

Tasting notes: airy crust with a bit of char, tomato sauce that is not too thick.

Churn ideas: try a 24 hour dough, a white pizza, or a "fridge clean-out" pizza night.

Photo ideas: cheese pull, flour dusted on the board, a half-eaten slice.

Rating: 3/5 (churn: dough needs work)

cooked by someone else

Lahmacun

Cooked by: the local place near home

Concept: thin, crisp, and rolled with parsley, onion, and lemon. Eaten standing up.

Tasting notes: crunchy edge, juicy minced meat, fresh herbs.

Churn ideas: ask what the dough is made with, or try the spicy version next time.

Photo ideas: rolled wrap cut in half, lemon squeezed over it.

Rating: 5/5 (keep)

Ramen at a friend's place

Cooked by: a friend, from scratch

Concept: a long broth that took most of the afternoon. Soft egg, green onion, and a lot of patience.

Tasting notes: rich broth, the egg was perfect, noodles a bit too soft.

Churn ideas: ask for the broth recipe, try making the egg at home first.

Photo ideas: the egg cut in half, chopsticks lifting noodles.

Rating: 4/5 (keep)

Mom's börek

Cooked by: my mom

Concept: layered yufka with cheese and parsley, baked until golden. A weekend tradition.

Tasting notes: flaky, salty cheese, the edges are the best part.

Churn ideas: ask her for the exact cheese ratio and write it down.

Photo ideas: a slice pulled up to show the layers, tea next to it.

Rating: 5/5 (keep forever)
